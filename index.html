About Ms. Karen

FAQ

Good things to know

What should my child wear?

Comfortable dance clothes, ballet shoes, tap shoes if needed, or anything your child can move in safely.

Do you offer drop-ins?

No, please see the PunchPass links above to purchase and reserve your spot.

Is this a serious studio environment?

Classes are structured and skill-building, but always playful, warm, and age-appropriate.

Will there be a recital?

A casual end-of-season sharing or mini-recital may be offered, depending on the session.
